## Cost-Saving Recommendations

1. **Bundle Services**: Combine hosting, domain, and email with one provider
2. **Annual Payments**: Pay annually for hosting to get discounts
3. **DIY Content**: Train staff for basic content updates
4. **Local SEO Focus**: Concentrate on Birmingham and West Midlands keywords
5. **Performance Monitoring**: Use free tools like Google Analytics and Search Console
6. **Bulk Material Purchasing**: Negotiate better rates with suppliers
7. **Equipment Maintenance**: Regular maintenance to extend equipment life
8. **Staff Training**: Invest in skill development to reduce subcontracting
